Welcome to Puck Reskin Designer

A few tips before you create your first rink.

Design your rink

Select layers or individual elements, then edit their appearance in Properties or try Presets. Apply your changes to keep them. You can also add a PNG graphic overlay.

Save to keep editing

Save creates an editable .rskn project you can open again later in this editor. Saving a project does not add your rink to Puck.

Export to use in Puck

Export PNG creates the finished rink texture. Choose Export to Puck for easy in-game use, or save the PNG elsewhere.

Using your rink in-game requires Toaster's Reskin Loader. Subscribe to the mod from the provided link, and then enable it via the in-game mods menu. With the mod enabled, open RESKIN MANAGER from main menu, navigate to Arenas tab, and select your rink reskin via the Ice selector.

Save project

Saving your project allows you to return to Reskin Designer later to continue editing. To use your rink in Puck, you must use Export PNG.
